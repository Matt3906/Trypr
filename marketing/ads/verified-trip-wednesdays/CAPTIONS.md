# Verified Trip Wednesday: captions and photo credits

Each folder is one carousel (7 or 8 slides): post all images in file order. The suggested dates are consecutive Wednesdays; the `#` on each cover is the episode number, so keep the order or re-render (`python3 -I source/build_verified.py OUT_DIR`).

## Credits

Photos are from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, and each photo carries its own credit chip on the slide. Maps are OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors) served by OpenFreeMap, credited on every map. The slide text and crops are changes to the photos, which is why the credits say "cropped". CC BY-SA photos ask that adapted versions are shared under the same licence; the on-slide credit plus the licence name covers the attribution requirement.
